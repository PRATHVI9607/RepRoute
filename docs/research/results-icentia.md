## Certificate validity (E2)

- **H2a** (V0, expected risk ≤ α on held-out bearings): threshold met, **not significant** (Holm-adjusted p = 1.00).
- **H2b** (V1, ≥ 90 % of folds within α): **not supported**.
- Risk is the safe-set loss (worst admissible policy) for V0 / V1, and the greedy policy's loss for V2, which only promises that policy. *Mean over folds* pools each fold's test windows; *mean over test units* first averages each bearing's windows, so every unit weighs the same. The CI resamples whole bearings (a bearing tested in several folds is one block).
- The H2a test (one-sided, mean < α) cannot reject by design for a method whose expected risk lands at α, as CRC's does, so H2a cannot be *supported* under the pre-registered rule (threshold met **and** Holm rejection) even when the bound holds.
- **H2a non-inferiority** (exploratory, ADR 0022; margin 0.01 fixed before it was computed; not in the Holm family): one-sided t-test over folds of mean V0 risk < α + 0.01, maximum p over α = **0.0546** (does not reject at 0.05). Mean risk α = 0.02: 0.0187, α = 0.05: 0.0469, α = 0.1: 0.0953.
- *Folds within α* is descriptive: CRC bounds the expected risk, so even under exact exchangeability about half of the folds (≈ 47 %) can exceed α.
