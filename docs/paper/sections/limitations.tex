\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Exchangeability.} The guarantee is for a test window exchangeable with the calibration
windows. Windows of one unit are autocorrelated, and a new unit may be shifted against the
calibration units. On Paderborn a fold has five calibration bearings, so at the level that matters,
the machine, the calibration sample is tiny; the window count overstates the evidence. We report
per-fold risk with unit-block intervals and treat H2 as an empirical finding across folds, never
as a theorem about new machines.

\paragraph{What the guarantee covers.} CRC, V0, the static set and the cascade bound the
\emph{expected} risk, not the risk of one deployment. Only \texttt{certified} decisions are covered,
counted per offered window; deferred, dropped and uncertified actions are outside the bound, and
deadline misses are SLO events. ACI and the drift monitor carry no finite-sample guarantee. The
cascade refuses both, so it has no drift protection; under a suspected shift the per-window
certificate with the audit loop is the only configuration with a shift response, and that response
has no finite-sample guarantee.

\paragraph{Agreement is not accuracy.} The certificate controls agreement with $\fraw$. Where
$\fraw$ is wrong, an agreeing representation is wrong too, and the certificate inherits every
error of the reference model. On Paderborn that model reaches a macro-F1 of 0.604 on unseen
bearings.

\paragraph{The Paderborn latent.} \rep{r6} on Paderborn runs $\fraw$'s fixed front-end on the edge;
its 0.7\,\% disagreement is largely by construction. The generic latents and engineered features do
not come close on unseen bearings.

\paragraph{Statistics.} The fold is the replication unit; folds of different repeats share test
units, so fold-level p-values are optimistic. Holm is applied within a dataset, not across
datasets. Fold-level bootstrap intervals over five folds under-cover. B4 and the profile-table
baseline screen representations marginally on calibration data and are uncertified. The cascade,
the static set, group CRC, the log-mel menu, the AUC gate, the non-inferiority test and the
noise-aware H6 rule were all added after seeing results and are exploratory; none replaces a
pre-registered verdict.

\paragraph{Group CRC needs units.} Its finite-sample term needs at least $\lceil 1/\alpha\rceil-1$
calibration units, which Paderborn and HAR do not have at any level and Sleep-EDF has only at
$\alpha = 0.1$. More units is the only remedy.

\paragraph{Hardware and measurement.} The edge is a development laptop, not a Raspberry Pi, so H4
is untested where it matters, and H6 and H7 compare the simulator with real sockets on the same
machine. Energy is modelled. Part of the cellular traces are synthetic; the measured 4G traces are
downlink throughput, used as an uplink shape. The Sleep-EDF real-socket test replays 60
windows per link rate. The sensitivity to 32 sensors per node rests on laptop duty-cycle timings.
Baselines after prior work (AdaEdge, conformal model selection, adaptive thresholds, two-mode
switching) are conceptual re-implementations adapted to this setting, not the authors' code.
