\section{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

\subsection{Datasets and splits}

Table~\ref{tab:datasets} lists the four datasets rerun with the final code. Every split leaves
whole units out: each fold assigns units to \texttt{train}, \texttt{ptrain}, \texttt{calib} and
\texttt{test}, and no unit is in two partitions. Paderborn~\cite{lessmeier2016paderborn} uses three
repeats of five bearing-disjoint folds (15 folds); the other datasets use five folds. Three further
datasets (HUST, UORED and PTB-XL~\cite{wagner2020ptbxl}) were run with earlier code, before the
fixes of the final rerun; they are reported for completeness
\ifarxiv in Appendix~\ref{app:earlier}\else in the extended version\fi{} and are not comparable
number for number. HAR, Speech Commands and Sleep-EDF were chosen by a screening that read their
own data, so they are not independent confirmations of the Paderborn results.

\subsection{Reference models}

Each $\fraw$ family was chosen by inner cross-validation on the training units of one fold and
frozen before any calibration or test partition was evaluated with it; every candidate is recorded.
Those units are test units in other folds, so the choice of family is not nested and test macro-F1
is slightly optimistic; within each fold the model itself is fitted on that fold's training units
only. On Paderborn it
is EnvNet: a fixed envelope-spectrum front-end of 96 values with no parameters, followed by a
standardised multinomial logistic regression. Larger models did not transfer across bearings. On
the other datasets the inner search chose a one-dimensional residual convolutional network on the
waveform. All reference models pass the gate fixed before evaluation (macro-F1 $\ge 0.40$ on test
units; Table~\ref{tab:datasets}).

\subsection{The representation menu}

The menu has nine representations: raw (\rep{r0}); delta plus zstd lossless (\rep{r1}); two
bounded-lossy wavelet codecs (\rep{r2a}, \rep{r2b}); engineered spectral features (\rep{r3},
128~B); a statistical summary (\rep{r4}, 24~B); a task-agnostic autoencoder latent (\rep{r5},
32~B); a supervised bottleneck (\rep{r6}, 16~B); and a vector-quantised latent (\rep{r7}). Feature
and latent heads are standardised logistic regressions or LightGBM models chosen with the same
search budget. An exploratory second menu adds two coarse log-mel representations (\rep{r10a},
258~B; \rep{r10b}, 146~B) with a small convolutional head on Speech Commands\expl.

\paragraph{What the 16-byte latent is on Paderborn.} On Paderborn, \rep{r6} is $\fraw$'s fixed
front-end followed by a learned 16-value projection. The edge therefore computes the reference
model's features, which is close to classifying on the edge, and \rep{r6} agrees with $\fraw$
largely by construction. A generic 16-byte latent could not follow EnvNet on unseen bearings. Any
statement that 16 bytes are ``sufficient'' on Paderborn must be read as ``the edge ran the cloud
model's feature extraction''. On HAR, Speech Commands and Sleep-EDF, \rep{r6} is a generic
supervised bottleneck that does not share $\fraw$'s front-end.

\subsection{Pre-registered hypotheses}

Table~\ref{tab:hypotheses} lists the seven hypotheses, frozen before any evaluation experiment was
run on Paderborn. Every later addition is logged with what had been seen before it; none changed a
metric, threshold or definition. A tested hypothesis (H1, H2a, H3, H5) is \emph{supported} only if
its threshold is met \emph{and} a Holm--Bonferroni procedure at family-wise level 0.05 over the
eight hypotheses rejects its null; we call it \emph{met} when only the threshold is reached. H2b,
H4, H6 and H7 have no replication unit and are descriptive; they enter the Holm family with
$p = 1$.

\subsection{Statistics}

The replication unit is the fold, as pre-registered. One-sided t-tests over folds give the
p-values. Folds of different repeats share test units, so these p-values are optimistic. Holm is
applied within one dataset, never across datasets. Confidence intervals on certificate risk
resample whole units (a bearing tested in several folds is one block). Per-fold bootstrap intervals
over five folds under-cover and should be read as rough. We report effect sizes and intervals for
every hypothesis whatever the test outcome. Edge timings are from a development laptop; energy is
modelled, never measured.
